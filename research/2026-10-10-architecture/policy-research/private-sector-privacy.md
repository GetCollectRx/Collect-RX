## Framing assumptions (state these to counsel)

- CollectRx acts for client businesses (Ontario dental first). For claim data it is most likely a service provider / processor for the client, who stays accountable (PIPEDA Sched. 1 cl. 4.1.3, VERIFIED; Alberta PIPA s.5(2), VERIFIED; Quebec s.18.3 mandate, VERIFIED text of S.Q. 2021 c.25 s.115).
- CollectRx becomes an accountable organization in its own right for anything it uses for its own purposes (cross-tenant carrier learning, product analytics, model improvement). That is the purpose limitation pressure point (section 4 below).
- Dental practices in Ontario are also health information custodians under PHIPA. PHIPA is out of scope here, but it likely governs the patient data more strictly than PIPEDA. UNVERIFIED (not analysed).
- PIPEDA applies to commercial activity across Canada and to cross-border flows; Alberta, BC and Quebec statutes apply to intra-provincial private-sector activity. The exact reach rule (s.26 substantially similar orders) was not read. UNVERIFIED. Treat all four regimes as potentially applicable to a national service.
- Carrier representatives: PIPEDA s.4.01 excludes business contact information collected "solely for the purpose of communicating" with the individual in relation to their business (VERIFIED). Rep names plus call content beyond contact purposes (for example stored behaviour notes) may fall outside that exclusion. Ask counsel.

## 4. Cross-tenant learning and purpose limitation

What the code does (files read):
- `src/server/learning/carrierLessons.ts`: after a call, a small Anthropic model (claude-haiku-4-5) reads `CallAttempt.transcriptText` (already redacted) and proposes lessons about carrier behaviour. Prompt forbids patient names, DOBs, policy and claim numbers, phones, amounts. Lessons are stored PROPOSED, human reviewed, and only APPROVED lessons are injected into future calls. Model `CarrierLesson` has no practiceId; `sourceCallAttemptId` keeps provenance.
- `src/server/learning/humanAssistedProfiles.ts`: synthesizes per-carrier profiles across `HumanAssistedCallLog` rows from every practice (keyed by carrier).
- `src/server/learning/practiceCarrierFeed.ts`: shows APPROVED lessons to any practice (no practice filter on the lesson query).
- `src/server/learning/submissionChannelMemory.ts`: stores what a carrier rep said about resubmission destinations, keyed per carrier.

Analysis (all reasoning, not a legal finding):
- Strong design points: lessons describe the carrier, not the patient; human approval gate; no tenant key in output rows. This supports an argument that the shared output is not personal information about patients and may qualify as anonymized or never identifying.
- Remaining exposure under purpose limitation (PIPEDA cl. 4.2.4, 4.5.1; Quebec s.12, s.23):
  1. The input to learning is a transcript derived from patient related calls. Using it to improve CollectRx's own product is a secondary purpose of CollectRx, not of the client. Without client contract language and, for identifiable data, consent, this is the weakest link. The processing of transcripts by Anthropic in the US is itself a transfer (rows 2 and 3).
  2. Redaction is incomplete by its own comments (`transcriptRedaction.ts`); free text lessons could re-embed identifiers or practice identity. No automated post check on lesson text was found.
  3. Provenance link `sourceCallAttemptId` ties a shared row back to a tenant's call, so a deletion or offboarding request cannot cleanly remove influence, and a Quebec practice's call could seed lessons used for all.
  4. `HumanAssistedCallLog` stores `repName`, `referenceNumber`, `amountStatedByRep`, and `callSummary` free text for synthesis across tenants.
  5. Cross-tenant leak risk: lessons shown to every practice, including competing businesses; a lesson might reveal one tenant's workflow.
- Controls to code: tenant level `learningContribution` flag and `learningConsumption` flag; contract clause version recorded; automated PII and tenant fingerprint scan on lesson text before it reaches PROPOSED; k-anonymity style threshold (publish only patterns seen across at least N tenants, N for counsel); expiry of provenance links after review; quarantine of Quebec tenants until counsel sign off; deletion test that tenant removal yields zero retained rows.
- Acceptance tests listed in row 15. Source check: Anthropic transcript handling and retention terms were not read (UNVERIFIED).
