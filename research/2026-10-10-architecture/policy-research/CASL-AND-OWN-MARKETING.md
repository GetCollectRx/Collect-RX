# L2a. CASL and CRTC rules for CollectRx's own marketing

Date: 2026-10-13. Status: research record, not legal advice, not reviewed by a lawyer. Positions are the founder's to sign (INTERPRETATION-AND-RISK-REGISTER.md). Evidence: EVIDENCE-LOG-2026-10-13.md (E1 to E6). Tags: VERIFIED (primary text read this session), EXCERPT, UNVERIFIED.

Scope: messages and calls CollectRx sends to prospects for itself, from `Collect-RX-main/src/server/marketing/` (email campaigns, prospect harvesting and enrichment, AI voice sales calls via `vapiSalesCall.ts`). Carrier calls are in CRTC-UTR-CARRIER-AND-SALES-CALLS.md.

## Which regime governs which channel

| Channel | Regime | Basis | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email, SMS, social DMs to a prospect | CASL s.6 | s.1(1) "electronic message means a message sent by any means of telecommunication, including a text, sound, voice or image message"; s.1(2) commercial electronic message (CEM) | VERIFIED |
| Live two-way voice between individuals, and voice recordings to a telephone account | Not CASL s.6 | s.6(8)(a), (c) | VERIFIED |
| AI voice calls (synthesized voice, automatic dialling) | CRTC Unsolicited Telecommunications Rules (UTR) | Telecommunications Act s.41(1), s.41(2) carves out only CEMs to which CASL s.6 applies; UTR Part I ADAD definition covers "pre-recorded or synthesized voice" | VERIFIED (text); whether an LLM agent is an ADAD is open (CRTC 2026-132 Q2) |

## Reading adopted for drafting (founder to sign)

- Email: proceed only on a recorded consent basis; conspicuous publication must be evidenced by URL. Guessed addresses and personal addresses from enrichment services are not used.
- AI voice sales calls: treat as telemarketing via an ADAD. They stay off unless an express ADAD consent record exists for the number, and calls to dental offices stay off until the founder signs a reading on "healthcare facility". Inbound demo calls (the prospect calls CollectRx) are not unsolicited telecommunications and are the recommended channel.
- Contrary reading: an LLM conversational agent is not an ADAD, and a dental office is not a "healthcare facility" in the telemarketing sense. Plausible, untested, and the CRTC is consulting on both points now.
