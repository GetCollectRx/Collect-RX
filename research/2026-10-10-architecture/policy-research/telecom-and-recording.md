## 2. Questions for counsel

1. Is transcription of a call by Vapi (and storage by CollectRx) an "interception" under s.183 and s.184, and does the practice's role as originator in autonomous mode supply s.184(2)(a) consent? Does that change for human-assisted mode where staff are the party and the carrier rep is not notified?
2. The Claims_Agent opener says "may be recorded for quality purposes" while recording is disabled. Does a false or inaccurate recording statement create exposure, and should it be removed or made true?
3. Is a Vapi LLM-driven voice agent an "automatic dialing-announcing device" under the ADAD Rules, given that it dials automatically and plays synthesized speech but also converses?
4. Are calls to insurer provider lines about claim status "telemarketing" or "unsolicited telecommunications" at all? If not, which UTR parts (if any) apply, and does the business-to-business position differ for ADAD?
5. For CollectRx's own outreach to dental practices (Group C): which UTR parts apply, what internal do-not-call list duties and deadlines apply, and do consent, hours, and identification rules apply to AI-voiced prospect calls?
6. Who is the "originator" for identification purposes: the practice or CollectRx? Which name, mailing address and number must be spoken?
7. Must the AI disclose that it is automated at the start of every call to a carrier rep, regardless of rule applicability? (Prompt currently says it will not claim to be human if asked directly.)
8. Status and likely direction of Notice of Consultation CRTC 2026-132; should we file anything or wait for the decision?
9. Retention: acceptable maximum period for call transcripts that include rep names and claim identifiers under PIPEDA and provincial health privacy statutes, and the legal basis for the default-off purge.
10. Quebec Law 25 and Alberta/BC PIPA: any extra notice or cross-border (Vapi, Twilio, model providers in the US) disclosures for call transcripts?
11. Is a recipient-time-zone calling window required by law for non-telemarketing calls, or only a business practice?
12. Voicemail: if an agent ever leaves a message, what identification and content rules apply?
