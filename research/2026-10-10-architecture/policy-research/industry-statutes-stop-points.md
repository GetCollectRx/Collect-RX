Applicability limit (VERIFIED as to text, UNVERIFIED as to application): Part I.1 duties are written between "owner" and "contractor". The Act defines "owner" (s. 1(1)) as a person having an interest in a premises at whose request, upon whose credit, on whose behalf, with whose privity or consent, or for whose direct benefit an improvement is made. An insurer funding a claim is not obviously an "owner". Whether a restoration firm invoicing an insurer is a "contractor" invoicing an "owner" is a question for counsel (section 2, Q1). Until answered, the policy pack should treat these dates as tracking aids and never as claims against the insurer.

Also read and relevant to restoration, not yet tabled: Part II.1 adjudication machinery (ss. 13.5 to 13.20, VERIFIED text), s. 6.3 (monthly invoicing; a clause conditioning invoicing on prior certification or owner approval is of no force, VERIFIED), s. 6.2 (payment duty subject to holdback). The prescribed form of the notice of non-payment is in a regulation not fetched (UNVERIFIED which regulation).
