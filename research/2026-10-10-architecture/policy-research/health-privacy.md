## 0. Key conclusions (read first)

1. PHIPA binds the health information custodian (HIC), not CollectRx directly, EXCEPT where CollectRx is (a) an agent (s. 17(2), s. 17(4)), (b) a non-agent electronic service provider (s. 10(4), O. Reg. 329/04 s. 6(1)), or (c) any person (s. 34 health numbers, s. 11.2 de-identified data, s. 72 offences). VERIFIED.
2. Agent status is defined by acting "for or on behalf of the custodian ... for the purposes of the custodian, and not the agent's own purposes" (s. 2 "agent"). VERIFIED. Any CollectRx use of PHI for its own purposes (cross-tenant learning, model improvement, marketing) is outside the agent definition and is not authorized by s. 17. The only prescribed exception to s. 17(2) in O. Reg. 329/04 s. 7 is for CMPA and HIROC. VERIFIED. De-identified data is not PHI, but s. 11.2 bars re-identification. VERIFIED.
3. Customer groups are NOT all PHIPA custodians:
   - Dental, physio, chiro, audiology, optometry etc: members of an RHPA college who provide health care are "health care practitioners" (s. 2 (a)); custodian para 1 of s. 3(1). VERIFIED text. Dentists being RHPA members is UNVERIFIED here (RHPA Sched. 1 not read).
   - Pharmacy: custodian under s. 3(1) para 4 iii. VERIFIED.
   - Non-RHPA rehab, hearing-aid dispensers, equipment vendors: custodian only if "primary function is to provide health care for payment" (s. 2 (d)). VERIFIED text; application to each vendor type is a counsel question.
   - Property restoration, collision/tow/storage: not health care providers; PHIPA does not make them custodians. Their claim files can still hold injury or health details (auto bodily injury, occupant info). Governing law is then PIPEDA or provincial PIPA. UNVERIFIED (PIPEDA text not read).
4. Disclosure to a private insurer is a disclosure to a non-custodian: consent must be express, not implied (s. 18(3)(a)) and knowledgeable (s. 18(5)). VERIFIED. The payment permission in s. 38(1)(b) covers only the Minister, another HIC, or the Agency, so it does not cover private insurers. VERIFIED. s. 37(1)(i) permits USE for obtaining payment or processing claims. VERIFIED. Practical consequence: each practice must hold the patient's express consent covering insurer communications, and CollectRx should not assume it.
5. Out-of-Ontario disclosure (US-hosted Vapi, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, Anthropic; hosting locations UNVERIFIED) is permitted by s. 50(1) if the individual consents, or the disclosure is "reasonably necessary for the administration of payments in connection with the provision of health care ... or for contractual or legal requirements in that connection" (s. 50(1)(f)). VERIFIED text. Whether sub-processor transfers fit (f) is a counsel question.
6. REPO DOC ERRORS found (VERIFIED against statute text):
   - `Collect-RX-main/docs/compliance/PHIPA-MANUAL-PROCESS-RUNBOOK.md` cites "PHIPA ss.65-68, 14-day deadline" for breach notice. PHIPA ss. 65-68 are civil damages, enforcement, Commissioner delegation and Commissioner collection limits. There is no 14-day period in s. 12(2)-(3) or O. Reg. 329/04 s. 6.3. The real rule is "at the first reasonable opportunity" (s. 12(2)(a), reg. s. 6.3(3)). The same runbook cites "ss.37-39" for deletion; those are use and disclosure permissions, not a deletion right. PHIPA has no general erasure right; it has access and correction (Part V) and s. 13 secure disposal. VERIFIED.
   - `Collect-RX-main/docs/compliance/HIA-Alberta-Clause.md` says "HIA Section 66" governs affiliates. s. 66 is the information manager agreement (s. 66(2) written agreement required; s. 66(1) repealed 2025 c21 s7(47)). Affiliate duty to notify is s. 60.1(1). "Affiliate" includes "an information manager" and persons under "contract or agency relationship" (s. 1(1)(a)). VERIFIED.
   - `Collect-RX-main/docs/compliance/Quebec-Law25-PIA.md` states patient identifiers are "never transmitted to voice agents". `PHI-VAPI-BOUNDARY.md` (Option B) and `src/vapi/client.ts` send patient_name and patient_dob to Vapi as `variableValues`. Direct contradiction. VERIFIED in code.
   - `PHI-VAPI-BOUNDARY.md` refers to "BAA" (a US HIPAA instrument). PHIPA analogue is a written agreement with the custodian (reg. s. 6(3) para 7 is only mandatory for health information network providers) plus agent conditions under s. 17(1.1). Terminology fix needed.
   - `src/services/pii-vault.ts` comment says residency headers satisfy Alberta HIA and Law 25. `LLM_RESIDENCY_HEADERS` has no repo callers outside the audit agent string check (grep). Vendor support of those headers is asserted, not shown. HIA and PHIPA contain no residency header concept. UNVERIFIED claims.

## 2. Questions for counsel

1. Is CollectRx an "agent" of each Ontario custodian (s. 2 definition), a non-agent electronic service provider (s. 10(4)), or both for different functions? What written instrument should exist per practice (terms of service addendum vs. standalone agent agreement)? Does reg. s. 6(3) para 7 content apply by analogy?
2. Are Vapi, Twilio, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, Anthropic, Google (Gemini in marketing/agents) agents of CollectRx, agents of the custodian, or non-agent service providers? PHIPA s. 17 does not address sub-agents expressly in the text read. What contract terms and what notice to custodians are required?
3. Is consent for an AI voice agent to disclose identifiers to an insurer covered by the consent already on a standard claim form? Is "knowledgeable" consent (s. 18(5)) satisfied if the patient was not told an automated system may call? Should the practice attest in-product?
4. Do transfers to US-hosted sub-processors fit s. 50(1)(f) (payment administration) or require consent under s. 50(1)(a)? Is a Canadian-region requirement advisable for marketing even if not legally required?
5. Cross-tenant carrier "lessons" and playbooks built from redacted transcripts: are these de-identified for PHIPA purposes (s. 4(2) "reasonably foreseeable ... utilized, either alone or with other information, to identify")? Does the learning use fall outside the agent definition (own purposes)? Is a custodian opt-in needed?
6. Which of the extended customer groups are custodians (s. 2 (d) "any other person whose primary function is to provide health care for payment")? Hearing-aid dispensers, mobility/medical equipment vendors, non-regulated rehab, disability assessors. For auto rehab, WSIB/FSRA/insurer-directed work, who is the custodian?
7. For restoration and collision: if bodily-injury or medical details appear, is PIPEDA (federal) engaged for the commercial activity, with breach-of-security-safeguards notification to the OPC? (UNVERIFIED here.)
8. Breach protocol: confirm no 14-day statutory timeline applies in Ontario for CollectRx or custodians, and what contract SLA CollectRx should offer practices. Confirm PIPEDA breach rules for non-custodian customers.
9. Is s. 10.1 (electronic audit log) in force as of today? The consolidation shows it as not yet proclaimed. Build to the requirement anyway?
10. Retention: no fixed period in PHIPA. Which period should the default use for dental and each RHPA college? Should purge be on by default for new tenants?
11. Recorded calls: Vapi transcripts and summaries. Is turning off recordings sufficient, given transcripts exist? Does Vapi "HIPAA mode" or equivalent (UNVERIFIED, not read) change retention? What vendor evidence should we hold?
12. Alberta: confirm information manager agreement (HIA s. 66) is the right instrument and whether the Alberta custodian's PIA obligation needs an amendment for AI voice. Confirm accuracy of OIPC "AI scribe" statement in `HIA-Alberta-Clause.md`.
13. Quebec: do outbound calls to insurers constitute communication outside Quebec requiring a PIA per transfer?
14. Does the call-opening disclosure ("automated assistant calling on behalf of X") satisfy any IPC expectation on transparency to patients (patients are not on the call)?
