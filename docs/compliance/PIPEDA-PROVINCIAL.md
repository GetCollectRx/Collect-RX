# PIPEDA / Canadian provincial privacy (P5-07)

**Not legal advice.** Healthcare and the private sector may be covered by **PIPEDA** or a **provincial** statute (e.g. Alberta PIPA, BC PIPA, Quebec Law 25). You must name your regime with counsel.

**Minimum program artifacts**

1. Jurisdiction and legal basis (which law(s) apply to your processing).
2. Breach / incident process (when to notify regulators or individuals) — see your counsel’s template.
3. If applicable: a contact for **access** and **correction** requests from individuals.

Store policies outside git if you prefer; link them from the internal security wiki.
