# CollectRx Terms of Service — DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW

> **STATUS: DRAFT. Not published, not binding. Requires review by Canadian counsel before any practice signs up under it.** Bracketed items need business decisions. Prepared 2026-07-18.

## 1. The Service
CollectRx provides software that automates insurance accounts-receivable follow-up for Canadian dental practices, including automated telephone calls to insurance carriers on the practice's behalf, claim-status tracking, cost estimation, and reporting (the "Service"). CollectRx is a software provider; it is not a collection agency, billing agent, insurer, or provider of legal, financial, or clinical advice.

## 2. Accounts and Eligibility
The Service is offered to businesses only (dental practices and their authorized staff), not consumers. The person creating a practice account represents that they are authorized to bind the practice. The practice is responsible for its users' actions and for keeping credentials confidential.

## 3. Subscriptions, Trials, and Billing
- Plans, included calling minutes, daily caps, and overage rates are as displayed at purchase (currently Core, Growth, and Scale tiers, billed monthly in CAD via Stripe).
- Trials: [30] days with limited included minutes; the Service stops placing calls when trial limits are reached.
- When included minutes are exhausted on a paid plan, calling pauses until the practice expressly confirms overage billing at the displayed per-minute rate; unconfirmed overage offers expire.
- Fees are non-refundable except as required by law. [Refund/proration policy — business decision.]
- CollectRx may suspend calling for non-payment.

## 4. The Practice's Responsibilities
- Provide accurate patient, claim, and insurance data, and only data the practice has the legal right to share for the purpose of claims follow-up.
- Confirm that use of automated calling for its claims is permitted under its carrier agreements.
- Designate a contact for escalations; respond to items the Service flags for human review.
- Not use the Service to place calls unrelated to insurance claim follow-up.

## 5. Automated Calls; No Outcome Guarantee
Calls are made by an automated system that identifies itself as automated in accordance with CRTC requirements. Carriers may decline to interact with automated callers. CollectRx does not guarantee any particular recovery amount, response time, or claim outcome; the Service's outputs (statuses, estimates, summaries) are informational and depend on carrier-provided information.

## 6. Personal Health Information
The practice remains the health information custodian (or equivalent) for patient information. CollectRx acts as a service provider processing information on the practice's instructions to provide the Service, as described in the Privacy Policy and [Data Processing Schedule — to be attached]. The practice is responsible for its own patient notices and consents required to use a third-party service provider for claims administration.
