# Dental Insurance Claim Scenarios — Research Document

## Overview
This document catalogs common insurance claim scenarios that Canadian dental practices face. Each scenario includes:
- Claim type (preventive, basic, major, ortho)
- Common carrier questions/objections
- Typical resolution paths
- Denial/dispute reasons
- Status inquiry patterns

**Source**: Industry research, AAPD/CDA claims data, carrier submission guidelines.

---

## I. Preventive Claims (Cleanings, Exams, X-rays)

### P001: Routine 6-Month Cleaning
**Scenario**: Patient had cleaning on 2026-08-15. Insurance should cover 100%.
- **Carrier questions**: Patient eligibility, deductible status, frequency (2+ per year?)
- **Common issues**: Deductible applied (patient responsibility), claim under review (COB)
- **Status codes**: Approved, Pending (frequency check), Denied (frequency limit)
- **Rep response time**: 2-5 min
- **Agent task**: Confirm coverage %, deductible applied, payment amount

### P002: Fluoride Treatment Add-On
**Scenario**: Fluoride ($30) billed with cleaning. Question: is it covered or rider-dependent?
- **Carrier questions**: Is fluoride in plan? Age restriction? Rider status?
- **Common issues**: Missing rider, excluded service, approved at 50%
- **Status codes**: Approved, Approved (50%), Denied
- **Rep response time**: 3-7 min
- **Agent task**: Determine if fluoride is covered; if denied, flag for escalation

### P003: Annual Exams (Multiple in Year)
**Scenario**: Patient saw dentist 3x for exams in one year. Claim submitted for 3rd exam.
- **Carrier questions**: Frequency limit? (usually 2x/year). Was prior claim adjudicated?
- **Common issues**: Frequency violation, prior claim pending
- **Status codes**: Approved, Denied (frequency), Pending (reviewing history)
- **Rep response time**: 4-8 min
- **Agent task**: Verify frequency limits, check prior claims, escalate if frequency disputed

## II. Basic Claims (Fillings, Extractions, Root Canals)

### B001: Single Amalgam Filling
**Scenario**: Amalgam filling billed at $85. Carrier has 50% coverage for basic.
- **Carrier questions**: Tooth surface count? Was amalgam pre-approved?
- **Common issues**: Deductible reduces payment, amalgam vs. composite (coverage %)
- **Status codes**: Approved, Approved (50%), Deductible applied
- **Rep response time**: 2-4 min
- **Agent task**: Confirm coverage %, deductible status, final patient responsibility

### B002: Composite Filling (vs. Amalgam)
**Scenario**: Composite filling billed at $120. Amalgam alternative is $85.
- **Carrier questions**: Does plan cover composite at basic or cosmetic rate?
- **Common issues**: Covered at amalgam rate (patient pays difference), not covered
- **Status codes**: Approved (amalgam rate), Denied (cosmetic), Approved (100%)
- **Rep response time**: 3-6 min
- **Agent task**: Clarify plan rules on composite vs. amalgam; explain patient liability

### B003: Extraction (Simple)
**Scenario**: Tooth extraction billed at $150.
- **Carrier questions**: Medical necessity? (pain, severe decay). Pre-existing condition?
- **Common issues**: Approved, or requires clinical notes
- **Status codes**: Approved, Pending (notes requested), Denied (pre-existing)
- **Rep response time**: 3-5 min
- **Agent task**: Confirm coverage %, note requirements if pending

### B004: Endodontic (Root Canal)
**Scenario**: Root canal billed at $800. Claim submitted; status unknown.
- **Carrier questions**: Pre-auth requirement? Tooth viability? (retreatment?)
- **Common issues**: Denied (pre-existing), Approved (50%), Pending (viability assessment)
- **Status codes**: Approved, Denied, Pending (clinical review)
- **Rep response time**: 5-10 min
- **Agent task**: Determine if pre-auth was obtained; explain payment & any clinical hold

## III. Major Claims (Crowns, Bridges, Implants)

### M001: Crown (Post & Core)
**Scenario**: Crown billed at $1,200. Patient has $1,500 annual max.
- **Carrier questions**: Pre-auth obtained? Core material (amalgam vs. composite)? Building code (D2xxx)?
- **Common issues**: Pre-auth required (many carriers), limited to annual max
- **Status codes**: Approved, Pending (pre-auth), Denied (pre-existing/missing pre-auth)
- **Rep response time**: 5-15 min
- **Agent task**: Verify pre-auth, coverage %, apply to annual max, explain remaining benefits

### M002: Bridge (3-4 Units)
**Scenario**: Bridge billed at $2,400 (4 units). Patient on annual max plan.
- **Carrier questions**: Pre-auth? Individual tooth vs. bridge approach? Annual max remaining?
- **Common issues**: Exceeds annual max, pre-auth denied, partial approval
- **Status codes**: Approved (partial), Pending (pre-auth), Denied (max limit)
- **Rep response time**: 8-15 min
- **Agent task**: Apply to annual max, explain shortfall, coordinate with patient on liability

### M003: Implant (Single)
**Scenario**: Implant abutment + crown billed at $2,800. Plan has implant exclusion.
- **Carrier questions**: Is implant covered? Rider? Alternative to bridge?
- **Common issues**: Excluded (many plans), covered at 50%, requires pre-auth
- **Status codes**: Denied, Approved (50%), Pending (pre-auth)
- **Rep response time**: 5-12 min
- **Agent task**: Clarify implant coverage; if denied, explain alternatives

### M004: Partial Denture
**Scenario**: Partial denture billed at $1,600.
- **Carrier questions**: Pre-auth? Rider? Frame material (acrylic vs. metal)?
- **Common issues**: Covered at 50%, requires pre-auth, frame material affects coverage %
- **Status codes**: Approved (50%), Pending (pre-auth), Denied (missing pre-auth)
- **Rep response time**: 5-10 min
- **Agent task**: Confirm pre-auth status, coverage %, explain patient responsibility

## IV. Orthodontic Claims (Phase 3)

### O001: Comprehensive Ortho Treatment
**Scenario**: Ortho treatment plan billed at $5,500 over 24 months. Claim for first phase.
- **Carrier questions**: Lifetime max for ortho? Phase 1 billed, when phase 2? Age limits?
- **Common issues**: Lifetime max (often $1,500-$2,000), age restrictions (under 18 only), deductible
- **Status codes**: Approved, Approved (limited by lifetime max), Denied (age)
- **Rep response time**: 8-15 min
- **Agent task**: Confirm ortho coverage, lifetime max, track phases, explain payment schedule

### O002: Ortho Treatment — Deductible Apply
**Scenario**: Ortho treatment; deductible has not been met this year.
- **Carrier questions**: Deductible applies to ortho? Remaining deductible amount?
- **Common issues**: Patient responsible for deductible first, then insurance pays
- **Status codes**: Approved (after deductible), Pending (deductible confirmation)
- **Rep response time**: 3-6 min
- **Agent task**: Calculate deductible impact, explain phased payment

## V. Claim Status / Pending Issues

### S001: Claim Under Review (Needs Time)
**Scenario**: Claim submitted 5 days ago; status is "pending review."
- **Carrier questions**: When will it be adjudicated? Any missing info?
- **Common issues**: Standard processing (2-3 weeks), missing documentation, frequency check
- **Status codes**: Pending, Pending (more info needed)
- **Rep response time**: 2-4 min
- **Agent task**: Provide status, expected timeline, flag missing docs if any

### S002: Claim Needs Missing Documentation
**Scenario**: Claim submitted but carrier needs clinical notes or pre-auth form.
- **Carrier questions**: What documentation is missing? Deadline for resubmission?
- **Common issues**: Notes not uploaded, pre-auth form incomplete, claim not yet billed
- **Status codes**: Pending (docs requested)
- **Rep response time**: 3-5 min
- **Agent task**: Identify missing docs, explain resubmission process, timeline

### S003: Claim Previously Denied — Reconsideration Request
**Scenario**: Claim was denied (e.g., pre-existing, frequency). Practice wants reconsideration.
- **Carrier questions**: Reason for original denial? New clinical evidence? Clinical notes?
- **Common issues**: Pre-existing condition (hard to overturn), frequency limit (policy limit)
- **Status codes**: Denied, Pending reconsideration
- **Rep response time**: 8-15 min
- **Agent task**: Explain denial reason, conditions for reconsideration, next steps

### S004: Partial Approval (Amount Lower Than Submitted)
**Scenario**: Claim submitted for $800 crown; carrier approved $400 (50% coverage).
- **Carrier questions**: Confirm $400 is correct patient portion? Coverage %?
- **Common issues**: Patient expected full coverage, doesn't understand coinsurance
- **Status codes**: Approved (partial)
- **Rep response time**: 2-4 min
- **Agent task**: Confirm approval amount, explain patient responsibility, coverage %

## VI. Denial & Dispute Scenarios

### D001: Pre-Existing Condition Exclusion
**Scenario**: Crown claim denied because tooth had existing condition before coverage start.
- **Carrier questions**: Date of coverage start? Tooth condition at coverage start? Clinical evidence?
- **Common issues**: Practice disputes pre-existing determination, requests reconsideration
- **Status codes**: Denied (pre-existing), Pending reconsideration
- **Rep response time**: 5-10 min
- **Agent task**: Explain pre-existing rule, conditions for appeal, documentation needed

### D002: Frequency Limit (Patient Exceeds Allowable)
**Scenario**: Patient had cleaning in June 2026, now claiming another in September (3 months).
- **Carrier questions**: Plan frequency (2x/year typical). Prior claim adjudicated?
- **Common issues**: Denied (frequency), patient disputes medical necessity
- **Status codes**: Denied (frequency limit)
- **Rep response time**: 3-6 min
- **Agent task**: Explain frequency limit, confirm plan rules, discuss exceptions process

### D003: Missing Pre-Authorization
**Scenario**: Crown claim submitted without pre-auth. Carrier requires pre-auth for crowns.
- **Carrier questions**: When was crown done? Can post-auth be submitted?
- **Common issues**: Claim held pending pre-auth submission, may be denied if not obtained
- **Status codes**: Pending (pre-auth), Denied (missing pre-auth)
- **Rep response time**: 5-10 min
- **Agent task**: Explain pre-auth requirement, next steps (retroactive submission?)

### D004: Not a Covered Service
**Scenario**: Whitening claim denied; plan covers only restorative services.
- **Carrier questions**: Plan coverage scope? Any cosmetic rider?
- **Common issues**: Whitening typically not covered; confirm with plan documents
- **Status codes**: Denied (not covered)
- **Rep response time**: 2-4 min
- **Agent task**: Confirm coverage scope, explain denial, suggest plan review

## VII. Payment & Reconciliation Scenarios

### R001: Claim Paid; Payment Received
**Scenario**: Claim approved and paid. Verify payment receipt.
- **Carrier questions**: Payment date? Amount? Method (direct to practice, to patient)?
- **Common issues**: EOB provided; practice verifies against records
- **Status codes**: Paid, Reconciled
- **Rep response time**: 2-3 min
- **Agent task**: Confirm payment amount, date, reconcile to claim

### R002: Payment Delay (Expected 2 Weeks Ago)
**Scenario**: Claim approved 3 weeks ago; payment not yet received.
- **Carrier questions**: Payment date? Status? Any hold or review?
- **Common issues**: Payment in process, check not received, ACH delay
- **Status codes**: Paid (pending delivery), On hold
- **Rep response time**: 3-6 min
- **Agent task**: Investigate payment status, provide expected date, escalate if overdue

### R003: Partial Payment (Claim Amount > Paid Amount)
**Scenario**: Claim approved for $800; practice received $400.
- **Carrier questions**: Why partial? Deductible applied? Coinsurance? COB reduction?
- **Common issues**: Deductible applied, coinsurance rate applied, COB coordination
- **Status codes**: Paid (partial)
- **Rep response time**: 3-5 min
- **Agent task**: Explain discrepancy (deductible/coinsurance/COB), confirm amount is correct

## VIII. Coordination of Benefits (COB) Scenarios

### C001: COB — Secondary Insurance
**Scenario**: Patient has primary insurance (spouse's plan). Practice bills secondary.
- **Carrier questions**: Primary insurance company? Primary adjudication date? Primary payment?
- **Common issues**: Primary claim not yet processed, COB calculation complex
- **Status codes**: Pending (awaiting primary), Approved (after primary)
- **Rep response time**: 5-12 min
- **Agent task**: Verify COB eligibility, get primary claim info, explain coordination

### C002: COB — Birthday Rule (Dependent Children)
**Scenario**: Child has coverage under both parents' plans. Determine primary.
- **Carrier questions**: Parent birthdates? Which plan is primary per birthday rule?
- **Common issues**: Incorrect plan identified as primary, COB coordination delayed
- **Status codes**: Pending (verifying primary), Approved (with COB adjustment)
- **Rep response time**: 4-8 min
- **Agent task**: Explain birthday rule, identify primary plan, explain COB process

## IX. Special Scenarios (TELUS AdjudiCare, CDCP, etc.)

### T001: TELUS AdjudiCare Claim (TPA Clearinghouse)
**Scenario**: Claim routed through TELUS AdjudiCare (not direct to underlying carrier).
- **Carrier questions**: Underlying carrier (e.g., Manulife via TELUS)? TPA processing status?
- **Common issues**: TELUS adds processing delay, need to identify underlying carrier
- **Status codes**: Pending (TPA processing), Approved (via TELUS)
- **Rep response time**: 8-15 min
- **Agent task**: Route to correct underlying carrier, track TELUS processing

### T002: CDCP Federal Program Claim
**Scenario**: Patient qualifies for CDCP (federal dental plan, launched 2024).
- **Carrier questions**: CDCP eligibility? Services covered? Maximum ($1,500/year)?
- **Common issues**: CDCP setup recent; some carriers unfamiliar; coverage scope unclear
- **Status codes**: Approved (CDCP), Pending (eligibility verification)
- **Rep response time**: 5-12 min
- **Agent task**: Verify CDCP eligibility, coverage scope, maximum benefits remaining

## X. Dispute & Escalation Triggers

**When reps become defensive or confrontational:**
- Claim repeatedly denied, patient disputes it
- Practice disagrees with coverage interpretation
- Rep seems uninformed or contradicts prior approval
- Claim amount discrepancy

**Agent escalation rules:**
- If rep refuses to provide status → escalate to supervisor
- If denial reason unclear → escalate to review team
- If pre-existing dispute → escalate to appeals process
- If payment not received after 30 days → escalate to accounting

## XI. Carrier-Specific Patterns

### Sun Life (Largest Canadian carrier)
- Strict pre-auth requirements for major services
- Frequent frequency checks on preventive
- Clear deductible application
- Responsive reps (2-5 min typical hold)

### Canada Life
- Moderate pre-auth requirements
- Good coverage for basic restorative
- Common deductible questions
- Average hold time 3-7 min

### Manulife
- Evolving coverage (recent updates to ortho)
- COB coordination can be complex
- Moderate pre-auth needs
- Hold time 4-8 min

### Green Shield
- Often best coverage for ortho
- Less strict pre-auth
- Good claims processing speed
- Hold time 2-5 min

### RBC Insurance
- Restrictive pre-existing rules
- Moderate coverage scope
- Pre-auth for major work
- Hold time 3-6 min

### TELUS AdjudiCare (Clearinghouse)
- Adds processing delay (TPA model)
- Routes to underlying carrier
- Requires underlying carrier ID
- Longer hold times (8-15 min)

## Key Metrics for Testing

**Per-agent success criteria:**
- IVR_Navigator: Reaches claims queue 95%+ of the time
- Hold_Sentinel: Silent hold handling, no false interactions
- Claims_Agent: Status retrieved 80%+ of time, no hallucinations
- Escalation_Closer: Dispute logic sound, escapes handled correctly
- Resolution_Closer: Payment verification correct, claim closed safely

**Across all agents:**
- No carrier block signals
- No PHI leakage
- Appropriate escalations (not over/under)
- Clean handoffs between agents
- Accurate outcome classification
