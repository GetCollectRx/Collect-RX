Six Carriers, Six Different Hold Music — A Follow-Up Cheat Sheet

Canadian dental offices bill a handful of insurers repeatedly. Each runs a different IVR, a different adjudication clock, and a different escalation path. This guide is what your billing lead wishes was pinned above the phone.

Universal rules (all carriers)

  • Call Mon–Fri during business hours — after-hours IVR often loops without a rep.
  • Have claim number, subscriber ID, date of service, and provider number ready.
  • Log reason codes verbatim — they drive the next action (resubmit, appeal, COB fix).
  • Do not assume "no EOB yet" means still processing; pended claims look the same in your AR report.

Sun Life

Largest share of Canadian group dental. Private claims: status check after ~32 days. CDCP claims use a separate provider contact centre — calling the number on a patient's old Sun Life card often lands in the wrong queue. See our CDCP guide.

Canada Life

Common in Ontario and Western Canada. IVR depth varies by employer group size. Escalation often requires referencing a prior call ID — ask the rep to note the interaction.

Manulife

High volume for mid-market employer plans. Watch for annual maximum exhaustion on major restorative — status may show "paid" at less than billed without a clear denial letter.

Green Shield

Not-for-profit insurer with distinct plan designs. Pre-determination recommendations differ by employer — always confirm whether predetermination was required for major work.

RBC Insurance

Smaller share but concentrated in certain employer segments. COB with a spouse's plan is a frequent stall — verify primary vs secondary before repeated status calls.

TELUS AdjudiCare

Clearinghouse, not one insurer. Identify the underlying TPA from the group number prefix before dialing. Minimum follow-up window is day 21 (earlier than the day-32 norm for other carriers).
