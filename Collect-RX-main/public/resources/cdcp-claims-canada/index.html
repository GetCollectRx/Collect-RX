CDCP Claims Are Piling Up — Here's What's Actually Slowing Your AR Down

The Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) added a new federal payer to nearly every practice's claim mix. It runs on its own rules — a different reconsideration clock, a different fee ceiling, and pre-authorization requirements that don't exist with private carriers. Here's what to watch for, and how to keep CDCP claims from quietly aging into write-offs.

CDCP isn't a private carrier — it's adjudicated through Sun Life

The CDCP is a federal program, but day-to-day claim adjudication runs through Sun Life via a dedicated CDCP provider contact centre — separate from Sun Life's regular group benefits line. If your front desk is calling the number on a patient's old Sun Life card, they're likely in the wrong queue, which is one of the most common reasons CDCP claim-status calls go nowhere.

Where CDCP claims actually get stuck

  • Pre-auth gaps. A procedure that would auto-adjudicate under a private plan gets denied outright under CDCP if pre-authorization wasn't filed first — and that denial often arrives with little explanation.
  • The 60-day reconsideration clock. Unlike most private carriers, CDCP gives you a hard 60-day window to request reconsideration after a decision. Miss it, and the claim is closed — permanently, not just delayed.
  • Fee grid variance, not denial. A claim can come back "paid" but at ~90% of the federal fee grid rather than your billed amount. That's not a denial your team will flag — it just quietly reduces what's collected, claim after claim, unless someone's reconciling against the original estimate.

What this means for your front desk

Every CDCP claim now needs the same follow-up discipline as a private carrier claim — a status check if it's been sitting too long, a reconsideration filed within 60 days if it's denied, and a reconciliation pass to catch fee-grid variances. For a practice with a meaningful CDCP-eligible patient base, that's a second full follow-up workflow stacked on top of the six private carriers your team already chases.
